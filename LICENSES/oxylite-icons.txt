Oxylite Icons
=============

MODIFICATIONS BY THE PARISHBSD PROJECT
--------------------------------------

ParishBSD installs the Oxylite Icons theme as-is, without modification.
The theme is extracted from the upstream archive to the system-wide
icons directory, and an icon cache is generated for performance.

Installation is performed by `15-setup-desktop.sh`.
